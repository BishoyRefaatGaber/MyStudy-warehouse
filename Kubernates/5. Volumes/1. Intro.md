#### Why volumes are important
- Data persistence
- Shared storage

#### Types of volumes
- **Ephemeral volume** --> have a lifetime linked to a specific Pod. 
- **Persistent volumes** -->  exist beyond the lifetime of any individual pod.

####  How volumes work
- A volume is a directory, possibly with some data in it, which is accessible to the containers in a pod. 
- When a pod is launched, a process in the container sees:
	- Filesystem view composed from the initial contents of the container image.
	- Volumes (if defined) mounted inside the container.
